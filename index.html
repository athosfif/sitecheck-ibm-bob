Method / 02

Small scope.
Explicit evidence.

SiteCheck does not claim to replace a complete accessibility or quality audit. It makes three objective patterns visible, records the human decision, and runs the same checks again after a change.

  1. SC-001 Image missing alternative text
  2. SC-002 Form field missing an associated label
  3. SC-003 Internal link points to a missing destination
